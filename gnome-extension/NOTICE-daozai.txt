The companion on the closed island (gnome-extension/src/pet.js, the way it is drawn in
gnome-extension/src/companion.js, and the twelve expressions in gnome-extension/pet/*.png) is the pet of
Daozai, https://github.com/Renly1994/xiaotang-daozai (src/pet.rs, src/pet_expressions.rs,
assets/pet/expressions/), carried over to GNOME Shell. The pixel shift for OLED displays
(gnome-extension/src/oledShift.js), the weather's way of finding a place, and the looks Cream, Light glass and
Floating cloud (their palettes and the colour mapping in gnome-extension/src/skinRules.js, from
src/skin_paint.rs and src/glass.rs; the cloud's body and light in gnome-extension/src/island.js, from
src/fluid.rs, a skin by 啵, @hanhb123) follow Daozai too. Under this
